How the estimate is calculated

For each orientation leg: kWh = (system kW × leg %) × peak sun hours/day × days in month × performance ratio × orientation factor. The month's total is the sum across all legs.

Peak sun hours is a simplified stand-in for daily solar irradiance, interpolated from a small table of latitude bands rather than measured satellite data.

Performance ratio bundles real-world losses — inverter efficiency, wiring, heat, soiling, shading — typically 75–85% for a well-designed system.

Orientation factor penalises tilt away from the latitude's optimal angle, and azimuth away from true south (northern hemisphere) — so east/west legs come out lower than south-facing, even at the same tilt.
